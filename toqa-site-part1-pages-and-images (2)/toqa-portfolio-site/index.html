The situation

A medical facility in Saudi Arabia. When I started, the page lacked visual consistency, and bookings stayed low even with paid advertising.

What I did

  • Built a more organized and consistent content direction across social media.
  • Planned and followed up photography sessions to raise the quality of visual content.
  • Wrote informative content that highlights the doctors' expertise rather than relying on direct sales messages.
  • Adapted relevant trends to the medical field.
  • Wrote content for the newly launched website, including medical articles.
  • Helped keep one brand identity across the physical location and digital platforms.
  • Supported the complex's participation in conferences and collaborations.

Results

  • More patient bookings through social media and the website.
  • Bookings from the new website, which carries content I created.
  • A more consistent brand identity across the facility and its digital platforms.
  • A wider brand presence through conferences and collaborations.
  • Search ranking improved from #14 to #3 in the tracked results.

Workflow

A fixed rhythm keeps a month of content on schedule.

  1. ResearchTopics, competitors and the audience's questions.
  2. PlanA monthly calendar and a tracker with a post type and objective for each day.
  3. BriefContent, copy and visual direction for the designer or video editor.
  4. ProduceI follow design reviews and photography sessions and pass on revisions.
  5. PublishScheduling posts and uploading website content.
  6. MeasureAd and content performance, and what to change next.
